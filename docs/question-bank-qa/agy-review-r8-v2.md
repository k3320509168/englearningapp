## 三、批次系統性排版與結構補充說明

1. **後段批次 `explain` 欄位中英黏字與英文單字誤連寫**：
   - 第 23～31 批多數題目的 `explain` 省去了中英文之間的半形空白，並在壓縮空白時將獨立英文單字連寫成錯字（皆已逐題列於上方明細表）：
     - `speak: "a non sequitur"`：`explain` 誤寫為 `nonsequitur`（應為 `non sequitur`）
     - `desc: "【語用】The witness could hardly have failed to notice the red coat..."`：`explain` 誤寫為 `couldhardlyhavefailed`（應為 `could hardly have failed`）
     - `speak: "The exception is available to either player, but not to both in the same round."`：`explain` 誤寫為 `notboth`（應為 `not both`）
     - `desc: "【文法】The account is no less mediated for ___ told in the speaker’s own voice."`：`explain` 誤寫為 `ownvoice`（應為 `own voice`）
     - `desc: "【指涉】The review doubts whether the saving survives repeated replacement..."`：`explain` 誤寫為 `lowprice`（應為 `low price`）
     - `desc: "【文法】So narrow is the exception that invoking it ___ alter the ordinary rule."`：`explain` 誤寫為 `invokingit`（應為 `invoking it`）

2. **語用與論證題（`discourse`）修辭句型高度同構**：
   - 全題庫約有超過 150 題聽力與閱讀題採用相同的二元對比框架（`"X is not Y, but Z"`、`"X need not imply Y"`、`"X does not by itself establish Y"`）。單題邏輯嚴密，惟連續作答時語感較同質，後續擴充至 2,000 題時建議增加隱喻辨析、反語層次比較與修辭結構題型。
